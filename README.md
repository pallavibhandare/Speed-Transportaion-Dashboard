## Project Overview
The SPEED Transport Performance Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze transport business performance across revenue, operations, dispatch, and cost management.

The project provides business insights through KPI summaries, charts, and performance metrics to support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features
- **Revenue Analysis:** Revenue by sector, business vertical, state, and customer.
- **Profitability Tracking:** Monthly revenue and gross margin analysis.
- **Invoice Monitoring:** Invoice distribution by payment status.
- **Operations Performance:** On-time delivery percentage and monthly dispatch versus targets.
- **Cost Analysis:** Cost breakdown and maintenance expenses by type.
- **Fleet & Bus Leasing:** Annual turnover and bus lease revenue analysis.
- **Performance KPIs:** Total revenue, gross margin, trips, load moved, and on-time delivery.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Excel Charts and KPI Cards
- Data Analysis and Reporting
- Dashboard Design and Data Visualization

## Business Value
This project demonstrates how transport-related data can be organized and visualized to monitor key performance indicators, identify operational trends, analyze revenue and costs, and support business decision-making.
